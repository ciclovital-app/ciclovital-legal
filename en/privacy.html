CicloVital privacy policy

In force since: October 29, 2026

1. What CicloVital is

CicloVital is a phone app that helps you record your menstrual cycle and other wellbeing information. It is only for people aged 18 or over: the app asks for your date of birth to check this.

CicloVital needs no account: there is no email, password or sign-in. Your profile exists only on your phone.

2. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is CicloVital, Costa Rica. Contact: ciclovitalapp@gmail.com.

The processing is governed by Costa Rica’s Law 8968 on the Protection of Individuals regarding the Processing of their Personal Data and its regulations (Executive Decree 37554-JP), without prejudice to the rights granted by the law of the country where you live.

3. Legal basis and purpose

The legal basis is your consent, which you give when you create your profile (the box “I authorize the processing of my data as explained”), and the performance of the service you request by using the app, including a purchase. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your data from the app.

The purposes are: letting you record your cycle and well-being on your phone, showing you estimates and content based on what you record and, if you buy, confirming and managing your Premium purchase. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or hand it over.

4. What is stored and where

Everything you write and record is stored only on your phone: your date of birth, your name and country (optional), your cycle and records, notes, tags, lists, reminders, appointments, medications, your pregnancy diary with copies of your photos, your settings and, if you turn on the lock, an encrypted fingerprint of your PIN (never the PIN itself).

None of this is sent to CicloVital or to anyone else. The most sensitive data is kept in your phone’s secure storage.

5. What never leaves your phone

  • Your records, your profile, your diary, the copies of your photos and your settings.
  • Your backup: it is an encrypted file that you download and keep wherever you like.
  • No health data is used for advertising. There is no advertising in CicloVital, we do not sell your data, and the app does not collect usage statistics or crash reports.

6. The only internet connections

The app connects to the internet only in these two cases, and in neither does it send health data, profile data or diary data:

  • New articles: if this feature is active in your version, the app may request articles from a CicloVital server, at most once a day. It is read-only: it sends none of your data and no identifiers. The server receives, like any web page, the internet address (IP) of your connection. You can turn this off in Profile → Privacy and security.
  • Purchases: only if you buy, restore a purchase or already have Premium (to check that it is still active), the app talks to Google Play or the App Store and to RevenueCat, the service that confirms purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier, the internet address (IP), technical data about the app and the phone, and the purchase. It does not receive your name, email, profile or any health data.

Your phone’s store (Google Play or App Store) and RevenueCat have their own privacy policies, outside CicloVital’s control.

7. International transfer

RevenueCat and the stores (Google Play and the App Store) may process purchase information in other countries, different from Costa Rica and from yours. Only what is needed to confirm the purchase reaches them: an anonymous identifier, the internet address (IP), technical data about the app and the phone, and the purchase. Your records, profile, diary and health data are never sent. By buying, you understand and accept this transfer.

8. Phone permissions

CicloVital asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and explains why beforehand:

  • Camera: only when you tap “Take a photo” in your pregnancy diary.
  • Photo gallery: only when you choose a photo for your diary.
  • Fingerprint or face: only to open the app, if you turn on the lock.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. Your own phone schedules them and their texts are general, with no health data.

The app does not ask for location, contacts or microphone. If you deny a permission, the app keeps working without that feature.

9. Your diary and photos

Diary photos are saved as a copy inside CicloVital, in a private app folder; they are not saved to your gallery or to the cloud. The copy is re-encoded as an image without location or camera data. If you delete a photo, an entry or your data, that copy is deleted; the original photos in your gallery are not deleted.

10. PIN or fingerprint lock

It is optional and off by default. It protects entry to the app; it does not encrypt the stored data. CicloVital cannot recover a forgotten PIN: the only way out is to erase the app’s data, with two confirmations.

11. Encrypted backup that you control

From Profile → Privacy and security → My data on this phone you can download your data as a file encrypted with a password that you choose and that is never saved. Without that password nobody can open it, not even CicloVital. The file does not include the lock, the Premium status or the copies of diary photos. You can open it again with the same feature on this or another phone.

On iPhone, if you have iCloud backup turned on for your own phone, that backup may also include CicloVital data; your phone’s settings decide that.

12. Sharing

Something leaves your phone only when you share it, for example a report for your doctor, the monthly summary or the weekly card. You choose whether to share it and with whom; CicloVital does not send it on its own.

13. How to delete your data

You can delete your profile and all your data whenever you want, from Profile → Privacy and security → My data on this phone → Delete my data. It takes three steps, the data is erased from your phone (including the copies of diary photos) and cannot be recovered, except from an encrypted file you downloaded earlier.

Deleting your data does not cancel a paid subscription. To cancel it, do so in your store: on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CicloVital; on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CicloVital. If you leave Premium, your records are not deleted.

14. How long data is kept

Your data stays on your phone while you keep your profile or until you delete it. The purchase information held by the store and RevenueCat is kept according to their own policies.

15. Security

The most sensitive data is kept in the phone’s secure storage. If you turn it on, the PIN or fingerprint lock protects entry to the app; it does not encrypt the stored data. Your backup is a file encrypted with a password that only you know.

No system is infallible. Protecting your phone with its own screen lock also protects your data.

16. Minimum age

CicloVital is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not offer the app to anyone younger.

17. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to withdraw your consent. Because your data is on your phone, you exercise these rights directly in the app: you view and correct your records on the app’s screens, and you download them in an encrypted file or delete them in Profile → Privacy and security → My data on this phone.

For any question or request about privacy, write to us at ciclovitalapp@gmail.com. If you believe your rights have not been honored, you may go to Costa Rica’s Data Protection Agency of the Inhabitants (Prodhab) or to the data protection authority of your country.

18. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version in the app, which will be updated with it, and on our website, with its date of effect.

19. Contact

Email: ciclovitalapp@gmail.com
